# StillScript Confidential Transcripts — Confidentiality & Data Handling

StillScript is built for professionals who work with confidential material —
legal, medical, counselling, and journalistic — where that material must stay
under your control.

## Your audio is transcribed entirely on your own computer.

Recordings are processed locally by the built-in speech-recognition engine.
Your audio is never uploaded, streamed, or transmitted — not to us, not to any
third party. Transcription runs fully offline.

## No tracking of any kind.

StillScript collects no usage data, analytics, or telemetry. The application
does not report how you use it, what you transcribe, or that you are using it
at all.

## The internet is used for only one routine purpose — and it does not send your recordings anywhere.

The first time you use a transcription engine, the AI model is downloaded *to*
your computer. This is a one-time download onto your device; none of your
content is sent out. After that, transcription works with no internet
connection at all.

## The one exception — the optional AI summary — is entirely your choice.

StillScript includes an optional feature that can generate a written summary of
a transcript using Anthropic's Claude AI. If, and only if, you choose to use it:

- Only the transcript *text* is sent — never the audio recording.
- It is sent using *your own* Anthropic API key, under your own account and
  Anthropic's terms.
- It is strictly opt-in. If you never enable it, no transcript text ever leaves
  your computer.

For work under the strictest confidentiality — privileged legal material,
patient records, protected sources — you can simply leave this feature unused,
and StillScript operates entirely offline after the initial model download.

## In short

Your recordings never leave your device. StillScript tracks nothing. The only
content that can ever be transmitted is transcript text you deliberately choose
to summarise, using your own AI account.
